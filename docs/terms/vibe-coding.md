# vibe coding: where it came from

The term 'vibe coding' is credited to Andrej Karpathy in February 2025 and quickly spread through software engineering, education and clinical research as a name for conversational, AI-driven programming. It now mostly means creating software by describing intent to LLMs or coding agents, often with little or no review of the generated code, and it is increasingly debated for its security, maintainability and open-source risks.

906 papers in OpenAlex use this term (2019–2026); 158 sentences read.

## Eras

- **2025–2025 · Coinage and first definitions**: A newly named practice of programming by conversing with LLMs, defined in early reviews and quickly tried in education, medicine and prototyping.
- **2026–2026 · Mainstream practice under scrutiny**: A mainstream, often agent-driven, minimal-review development mode that is benchmarked for security, criticized for its effects on quality and open source, and framed as a stage to move past toward agentic or specification-driven engineering.
